🌳 Smart Piezoelectric Tree Energy Harvesting System

EcoGrow AI introduces an innovative renewable energy system where flexible piezoelectric materials are attached to tree branches. When wind causes natural branch movement, mechanical vibrations are converted into electrical energy. The generated electricity is stored in a battery and can be used for household applications.


🔋 Working Process

Wind Movement 🌬️ → Tree Branch Vibration 🌳 → Piezoelectric Material ⚡ → Rectifier Circuit 🔌 → Battery Storage 🔋 → Home Appliances 🏠




🎬 Tree Energy Device Demonstration

⚡ Demonstration of piezoelectric energy generation, IoT monitoring, and battery storage process.


🧩 Materials Used

  • 🌟 PVDF Piezoelectric Film - converts vibration into electricity
  • ⚡ Conductive Electrodes - collects electrical charges
  • 🔌 Rectifier Circuit - converts AC to DC power
  • 🔋 Rechargeable Battery - stores generated energy
  • 📡 IoT Sensors - monitor voltage, current and energy generation
  • 🌡 Environmental Sensors - measure wind and tree conditions
